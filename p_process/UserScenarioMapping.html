Scenario

She wants a subtle piece of jewelry or clothing that she knows relates to her favorite show, but doesn't outright give her away as a 'nerd'. She comes across an Instagram ad for a jewelry shop named Feux Verts, selling cute off brand necklaces for her favorite show.

Actor

Victoria

Motivator

Wanting "low key" merch for her favorite shows

Intentions

Victoria wants to be able to wear something subtle that shows her love for her favorite shows, while also supporting a small buisness.

Action

Victoria is looking online for low-key but retty necklace or other piece of jewlery when she comes across a Feux Verts Instagram ad, a locally owned small buisness that makes custom, fandom inspired jewlery.

Resolution

She places an order for her customized "Hexlace" from Feux Verts.
